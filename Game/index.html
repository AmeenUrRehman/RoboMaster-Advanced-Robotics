Four steps, one brain

1 Search

No tag in sight? Robo spins slowly on the spot and looks around. If it saw the tag last on the left, it turns left.

2 Line up

Robo sees the tag. It turns until the tag is in the middle of its camera, then waits a moment to be sure.

3 Chase

Robo follows a smooth speed plan: start gently, speed up, slow down gently. Never faster than 0.16 metres per second.

4 Stop

Robo stops 20 centimetres from the tag. All four wheel motors go to exactly zero volts.

The magic code

Robo's camera reads this black and white square. It is like a barcode that says "I am number 3". The triangle and the circle in the game are decoys: Robo ignores them.

The real tag family is called AprilTag (tag36h11). Tag 3 is printed 15 cm wide.

The speed plan

The plan is a cubic curve (a smooth polynomial). The chase time is T = 1.6 x (distance - 0.20) / 0.16, with at least 4 seconds. That keeps the top speed the same, 0.137 m/s, for every distance.
